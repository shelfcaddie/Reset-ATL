What Is Reset

Not a gym. Not a spa.
Something Atlanta has never had.

RESET is an exclusive social and professional wellness club in Atlanta where professionals can work, train, recover, and build community in one place, supporting both physical and mental well‑being. It's designed for people who want to stay healthy, grow professionally, and connect with like‑minded peers.

Locker Rooms & Recovery Amenities
Premium locker rooms with personal lockers, sauna, steam, cold plunge, high end products, and recovery amenities.
Performance Gym & Group Fitness
Strength, cardio, functional training, and group fitness classes supporting a wide range of fitness goals.
Restaurant & Bar
Fresh, nutritious, and affordable breakfast, lunch, and dinner options.
Work Spaces
Quiet workspaces, meeting rooms, and flexible seating.
Golf Simulators
Three Trackman golf simulators.
On-Site Partners
Vendor partnerships with local businesses such as IV therapy, grooming, nail salon, and other services under one roof.
